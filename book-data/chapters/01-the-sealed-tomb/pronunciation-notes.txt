THE FIRST CITY — CHAPTER 1 — PRONUNCIATION NOTES

These are the recommended CANONICAL AUDIOBOOK readings for this production.

Zik — "Zeek"
Reason: keeps the nickname naturally connected to Zikir.

Zikir — "Zee-keer"
Reason: gives the full name two clean syllables and keeps the nickname Zik/Zeek intuitive.

Ashur — "ash-SHOOR"
Reason: aligns well with the Mesopotamian/Akkadian Aššur tradition, with stress on the second syllable.

Kurash — "KOO-rash"
Reason: preserves the spelling used in the novel and fits the Kuraš-form family better than an English 'Cyrus' reading. Chosen for this audiobook's canon.

Enhedu — "en-HEH-doo"
Reason: keeps the visible En-hedu structure recognizable and is easy for English TTS to reproduce consistently.

Balag — "BAH-lahg"
Reason: close, simple reading of the Sumerian-looking form; broad first vowel, final hard g.

Bel-iddin — "BAYL-ID-din"
Reason: reflects long-vowel Bēl plus iddin while remaining intelligible to English listeners.

WATCH WORDS
The following ordinary English archaeological terms should normally be left alone because modern TTS handles them correctly:
kiln, jamb, lintel, awl, masonry, corrosion, geometric, mortar.

If Kokoro produces a bad reading on a specific selected voice, add a replacement only after auditioning that voice. Do not pre-phoneticize common English words unnecessarily.
